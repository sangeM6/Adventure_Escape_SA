Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find answers to common questions below.

What activities do you offer?

+

We offer adventure experiences such as hiking, kayaking, ziplining and rock climbing.

How much do activities cost?

+

Our adventure packages cost R1 500, while individual activities cost R750. If you book more than one individual activity, you receive a 10% discount on the activity total.

How do I book an adventure?

+

Simply browse our available packages or activities, select your preferred option and complte the bokking form on our website.

Can I book for a group?

+

Yes! We welcome booking for families, friends, schools and corporate groups.

What should I bring?

+

Bring comfortable clothing, closed-toe shows, drinking water, sunscreen and a hat.

How can I contact you?

+

Call us on +27 12 345 6789
