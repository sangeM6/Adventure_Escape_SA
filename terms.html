Terms & Condtions

Please read these terms carefully before
submitting your booking request.

1. BOOKING REQUESTS


Customers must provide accurate booking and contact
information. Submitting a booking request does not
automatically confirm a reservation.

2. PAYMENTS


Payment arrangements and any application charges must be
confirmed with Adventure Escape SA.

3. CANCELLATIONS AND CHANGES


Customers should contact Adventure Escape SA to request
booking changes or cancellations.

4. SAFETY


Participants must follow the instructions provided by activity
guides and comply with applicable safety requirements.

5. PERSONAL INFORMATION


Customer information must be handled responsibly and used
for appropiate booking-related purposes.

6. CONTACT INFORMATION


Customers may contact Adventure Escape SA for questions
about these terms and conditions.
